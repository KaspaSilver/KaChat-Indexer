## Fixed after the report (same day)

Every open item below was fixed, reviewed and merged on main. Commits:
- push e3ff3d2
- processor f393c00
- webserver and admin 66ee8a3, 9e1e32d, 625415d
- chat indexer cdd8dc2

The fixes were checked beyond unit tests (311 tests pass):
- The processor ran on a scratch copy of the live schema with the last hour's real K
  transactions. It stored the same rows as live.
- The webserver ran on a scratch copy of the live data. Its leaderboards match live
  byte-for-byte; `/broadcast-channels` now hides the KaPlay rooms, as intended.
- The profiles follower counted real fees from the live node.
- The chat indexer synced real traffic from the live node into a fresh store, with no errors.

What changed, per component:
- **Chat indexer:**
  - Sender lookups retry (2 s to 5 min).
  - purge-sender removes every row of a purged tx, and the read paths skip a missing payload.
  - Entries added after acceptance ask for their sender, and a tx whose sender was blocked
    is not stored again.
  - One acceptance record per tx, with no asserts. A watchdog exits the process when a
    processor thread ends.
  - Coinbase outpoints are written on acceptance, and unwatched outpoints are swept.
  - purge-all clears pending senders.
  - A payment filed under the sender's change moves to the recipient, for data indexed from now
    on.
  - `/import-file` validates rows and skips blocked senders.
- **Push:**
  - Stored primary addresses and KaPost keys are re-checked against the bound wallet at start.
  - Watch-only lookups use an in-memory index.
  - Register and update are rate-limited per device token.
  - The per-token maps are pruned.
  - `kaposts_pubkey` must be the signer's key.
  - The new-post subtitle is char-safe.
  - A panic in the registry is caught per command.
- **Processor:**
  - A signature is claimed only after every check, in the same transaction as the write.
  - Moderation tombstones (`kachat_removed_txs`).
  - Votes are last-write-wins.
  - Push cooldowns, a room-mention cap, and a name-lookup cache.
  - No pushes for a backlog older than 30 min.
  - Poll votes and follows count once per x-only key.
  - Switches and lists are loaded before catch-up.
  - KaPlay room rows are validated.
  - The retry path stays quiet.
  - A vote notification goes only to the real author.
  - Known chat kinds log at debug.
  - A panic costs one tx, not the worker.
- **Webserver and admin:**
  - Incremental, single-flight leaderboards that are never cached on error. The same goes for
    the Explorer.
  - `/api/settings` saves every field, then reports supervisor problems as a `warning`.
  - KaPlay rooms are hidden from `/broadcast-channels`.
  - Names and profiles endpoints reject another network's address.
  - Chat-import parses the address.
  - The blocklist purge also removes polls and translations.
  - Admin deletes write tombstones.
  - Poll and follower counts are by x-only key.

**Left as is, on purpose:**
- **No per-IP limit on push registration.** Every request reaches the indexer through the proxy,
  so the address would be the proxy's. It needs a trusted-proxy setting first. The per-token
  limit is in place.
- **Payments already filed under the sender's change before this fix stay there.** Moving them
  needs each tx's outputs again (a re-scan).
